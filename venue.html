About the venue

Mercure Salvador Rio Vermelho

Located in the heart of Salvador's most vibrant and bohemian neighborhood, this oceanfront hotel offers the perfect balance of focused learning and relaxation.

The venue features modern, well-equipped conference rooms, reliable high-speed Wi-Fi, and comfortable accommodations with ocean views. Outside of sessions, participants can network by the spectacular infinity pool or explore the rich culture and gastronomy of the Rio Vermelho district right at their doorstep.

How to get there

Travel made clear.

Salvador is well-connected both domestically and internationally. Here is how you can easily reach the hotel upon arrival.

By plane Salvador International Airport (SSA) is about 25 km away. The easiest and safest way to reach the hotel is via app rides (Uber) or official airport taxis, taking around 40-50 minutes.
By bus If arriving from other cities in Bahia or Brazil, the main Bus Terminal (Rodoviária de Salvador) is about 10 km away. A quick 20-minute taxi or Uber ride will get you to the venue.
Organized transfer Information regarding any official shuttle services or shared rides among participants will be shared closer to the event date.

Practical information

Before you travel.

Weather

Tropical climate

Salvador is warm year-round with temperatures usually between 24°C and 30°C (75°F - 86°F). Bring light, breathable clothing, sunglasses, and a light jacket for air-conditioned rooms.

Meals

Bahian cuisine

Breakfast is typically included at the hotel. For lunch and dinner, Rio Vermelho is famous for its incredible local food and diverse restaurants catering to all dietary needs.

Bring

Participant checklist

Don't forget your laptop, comfortable shoes, and sunscreen. Note that power outlets in Salvador are 127V (Type N), so bring an international travel adapter if needed.
