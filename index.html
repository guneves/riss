Programmed activities

Space to learn and connect.

The school combines academic sessions with social and cultural activities that encourage informal conversations and long-term collaboration.

Day 1 · 2 Dec

Foundations & Design

Dive into the Foundations of Quantum Software Engineering and explore Requirements and Design with experts Shaukat Ali and Elisabetta Di Nitto.

Day 2 · 3 Dec

Testing & Implementation

Focus on Quantum Software Testing in a probabilistic world and practical Programming and Implementation with Rui Maranhão and Mariia Mykhailova.

Day 3 · 4 Dec

Panel & Closing

Join an interactive discussion with invited lecturers on the upcoming challenges in Quantum Software Engineering, followed by concluding remarks.

Venue

A setting built for focus and exchange.
